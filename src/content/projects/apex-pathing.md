From March to August 2026 I led the Apex Pathing dev team. Apex is an open-source FTC path-planning and kinematics library. The people on the team wrote the SDK, the docs, and the visualizer. I reviewed the changes they landed and kept that work pointed at one library.

The site is [apexpathing.com](https://www.apexpathing.com/). The code is in the [ApexPathing](https://github.com/ApexPathing/ApexPathing) org.
