At HackGT 13 I built Familyr with Olivia and Nish. I owned the Next.js frontend and the Supabase and Postgres backend: schema, APIs, chat, and two-way Google Calendar sync.

Events pulled from chat require consent and an opt-out. Private messages stay out of the health reports.
